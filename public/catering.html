Catering & events

The catering years came first

Before the storefront, this was a catering kitchen — orders posted on Facebook Marketplace, packed and smoked for parties and events. The pop-up playbook still runs: tell us the event and the headcount.

What to expect

Same smoke, bigger trays

Ribs, pork belly, grilled chicken and house-made Hmong sausage — the same mesquite program the restaurant runs every day, scaled for a crowd. Kua txob travels with it.

Book the way the pop-ups did

Catering orders started here on Facebook Marketplace and grew by word of mouth. Calling or messaging the shop is still exactly how a date gets set — no portals, no forms-in-a-black-hole.

Ask for the day’s smoke

Every menu follows what went up on the grill that week. Tell us the date and headcount and ask what’s coming off the smoke — the answer is the menu.

Feeding 20 or 200 — start with a call or a note, and the shop will answer with what it can smoke for your date.
