Privacy

What this site knows about you

Short version: nothing tracks you here. The only information this site collects is what you type into the contact form.

Contact the shop

Contact form

The form on the contact page collects the name, phone, email and message you enter. The note is delivered to Sactown Hmong BBQ’s own email inbox so the shop can answer. Nothing is sold, shared or added to a marketing list.

Cookies & tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. There is no visitor counter and no third-party tracker.

Google Maps

The visit and home pages show a Google Maps embed. When those pages load, Google receives standard request data (IP address, browser information) as part of delivering the map. See Google’s own privacy policy for details on that request.

Hosting

Pages are served from a standard hosting provider, which logs ordinary web requests (page, time, approximate location) for reliability.

Questions

Ask the shop directly — same form, or (916) 822-4344.
