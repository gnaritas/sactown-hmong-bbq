From Facebook Marketplace to Franklin Boulevard

For years Sactown Hmong BBQ was the pop-up and catering kitchen — orders posted on Facebook Marketplace, weekend events, lines that kept growing. In October 2026 the shop opened its first storefront on Franklin Boulevard, and The Sacramento Bee called it Sacramento’s first Hmong barbecue restaurant.

Story details: The Sacramento Bee (Oct 22, 2026) and Abridged Press opening coverage.
